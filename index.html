When the alarm sounds

  1. Stop and leave everythingStop what you are doing. Leave your bags, books and phone where they are.
  2. Line up behind your teacherWalk quickly and quietly. Do not run, push or go back for anything.
  3. Follow your routeUse the green arrows for your classroom. On the 2nd floor, take the stairs for your block and stay to the right.
  4. Gather in the Sport YardStand with your class. Your teacher will take attendance. Tell your teacher at once if someone is missing.
  5. Wait for the all-clearStay in the Sport Yard until a teacher tells you it is safe to go back inside.
